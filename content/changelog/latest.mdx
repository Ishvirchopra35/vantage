Launch 1 brings the core workflow from job discovery to tracked applications.

## Included in this release

- ATS scoring for base and tailored resumes with score history and improvement tracking.
- Resume tailoring powered by job parsing and structured optimization suggestions.
- Cover letter generation aligned to job context and profile data.
- Application tracking dashboard with status updates and activity metrics.
- Blog content foundation with MDX support and frontmatter-based loaders.
