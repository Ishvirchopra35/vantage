Today is the day. Vantage is officially live and ready for you to use. Everything we set out to build is now in your hands at [vantage-ai.dev](https://vantage-ai.dev).

## What Vantage is

Vantage is your unfair advantage in the job search. It is built for university students and new graduates - the people sending out a hundred applications and hearing back from almost none of them. Vantage closes that gap by giving you the same edge that expensive coaches and paid tools give everyone else, in one place, for free to start.

It is not another chatbot. Every tool in Vantage knows your history: your resume, your past applications, how your resumes have scored, and what has worked for you. The more you use it, the sharper it gets.

## What is ready right now

The full workflow is live, start to finish.

- **Resume tailoring** that rewrites your resume for a specific job, with a clear per-bullet view so you can see exactly what changed and why.
- **ATS scoring** that shows how your resume reads to the automated systems most companies use to filter applicants, with the missing keywords and fixes spelled out.
- **Cover letters** written from the job description and your real background, in your own template if you have one.
- **Application tracking** so every job you apply to lives in one dashboard, from applied to offer.
- **Networking drafts** for connection requests, cold emails, and follow-ups.
- **Interview prep** with practice questions and feedback tailored to the role.
- **A job feed** that surfaces roles matched to your target titles.
- **A [Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/vantage-auto-fill/mgapanbbaplohlojbmghoglmpfpogook)** that pre-fills application forms with your profile, so you spend less time retyping the same details.

## Why we built it

Applying for jobs is broken for people just starting out. The advice is scattered, the tools are expensive, and the systems that decide your fate are invisible. You do the work, you send the applications, and you hear nothing back - with no idea what went wrong.

Vantage exists to fix that. It makes the invisible parts visible, does the repetitive work for you, and gets smarter every time you use it. The point is not to apply to more jobs. It is to apply to the right ones, well.

## Getting started

Head to [vantage-ai.dev](https://vantage-ai.dev), create an account, and upload your resume. Vantage reads it and fills in your profile automatically, so you can start tailoring your first application in minutes.

This is a beginning, not a finish line. We have a lot more planned, and we are shipping quickly. Thank you to everyone who tested early builds and told us what was broken. We built this for you.
